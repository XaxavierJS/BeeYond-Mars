## Assessment against judging themes

- Impact: clear educational aim; define audience and gather brief learner feedback.
- Creativity: narrative exploration and a biological mission provide a distinctive framing; show actual gameplay.
- Validity: biggest gaps are reproducible route evidence, legacy engine tests and broader user testing.
- Relevance: mission cycle fits the available summary; check the full challenge before claiming total compliance.
- Presentation: English submission text and public game exist; final demonstration and portal evidence remain outstanding.

These are audit judgments, not jury scores or additional official requirements.
